YOURZ – Sign In & Flight Search

UI/UX · Mobile app

RoleUI/UX design
ToolsFigma, Prototype
TypeUI/UX · Mobile app

Case study

YOURZ is a mobile app for signing in and searching for flights. The sign-in screen offers two options: email with password, or sign-in with a phone number. After signing in, the ticket screen opens with One Way and Round Trip tabs, and the search form ends with an orange Search Flight button. Results appear as ticket cards that show the route, flight time, airline and price at a glance. The light blue header and orange buttons contrast well, so the main action stands out.
